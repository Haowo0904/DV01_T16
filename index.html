02 / Behind the total

The increase was uneven across road users

The national total hides contrasting patterns. Compare three major groups first, then examine how every group contributed to the change.

Pedal cyclist cases grew faster than car driver and motorcyclist cases

Between 2011 and 2021, pedal cyclist cases increased 51.4%, compared with 21.7% for car drivers and 17.4% for motorcyclists. These are changes in counts, not injury rates.

04 / What the evidence tells us

A larger recorded burden, with different patterns beneath it

Recorded cases increased by 5,472 between 2011 and 2021. Pedal cyclists had the largest absolute increase (+2,772), followed by car drivers (+2,379) and motorcyclists (+1,318). Decreases in other groups offset part of these increases.

Age and sex composition add context to those patterns. They describe who appears in the hospitalisation records, but cannot establish who was most at risk or why the counts changed.

Population growth, travel exposure and reporting changes must be considered before interpreting these results as changes in safety. This story does not estimate the effects of policy, behaviour or the pandemic.

How to interpret these charts
  • Counts represent hospitalised cases, not crashes or fatalities.
  • Road user shares use the national total for that year. Age and sex shares use the total for the same year and road user.
  • Year comparisons subtract start counts from end counts. Percentage change divides that difference by the start count; a zero start has no defined percentage change.
  • Missing combinations remain “No record”; explicit zeros remain zero. Age bands have unequal widths.
  • Victoria’s 2012 and NSW’s June 2017 reporting changes affect comparability. Counts are not adjusted for population or travel exposure.

The original workbooks and extraction workflow are not included in this repository, so their exact worksheets and raw-data derivation remain unverified. See the processing and design record and usability evaluation protocol. Participant testing is pending.
